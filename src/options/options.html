KeyNav

Site rules

Turn KeyNav off on a site, or let the site keep some of its own keys. Leave pass keys empty to turn KeyNav off there. Patterns accept *: mail.google.com, *.google.com, https://example.com/app/*.

Key mappings

One per line: map gt nextTab, unmap x, unmapAll. Lines starting with # are comments. Press ? on any page to see your keys.
